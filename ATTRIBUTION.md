# Attribution requirements

Tailnet IRL Server may be used, modified and redistributed under the terms in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). Every redistribution must keep the following acknowledgement
in a prominent and reasonably visible location:

> This product includes Tailnet IRL Server software developed by Nik Pottbecker (https://github.com/nikpottbecker/tailnet-irl-server).

## Where to place it

- **Source repositories and forks:** near the top of the README and in the retained license file.
- **Desktop or mobile applications:** in the About, Credits or Legal screen, reachable from the primary navigation.
- **Hosted services:** on an About, Credits or Legal page linked from the main interface or site footer.
- **Command-line tools:** in `--about`, `--version`, documentation or another place users can reasonably find.
- **Advertising or promotional material that mentions the software or its features:** directly in that material or in an immediately visible credits section.

The acknowledgement must not be hidden only in package metadata, commit history or an unrelated dependency lockfile.
